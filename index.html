📖 Competition Methodology & Mathematical Foundations

Full documentation of statistical models (Elo, Pythagorean, Poisson, Bayesian shrinkage, Monte Carlo, LightGBM, Ensemble), risk management parameters, execution slippage models, bankroll accounting, environment segregation rules, and zero-hallucination compliance.

1. Zero-Lookahead Walk-Forward Backtesting (Spec §17) — 28,072 Games, 2015-2026

Every betting strategy operates in strict chronological sequence. When evaluating game $t$, models are provided only with information that existed prior to first pitch: rolling team ratings derived from games $1, \dots, t-1$, verified gameday lineup announcements, starter FIP, and pre-game NOAA weather observations. No future outcomes or post-game statistics leak into decision timestamps. Uncompleted 2026 games produce OPEN/PROPOSED signals, never retrospective settlements.

2. FiveThirtyEight Calibrated MLB Elo Engine + Margin-of-Victory

Team power ratings are calibrated using dynamic margin-of-victory Elo:
P(Home Win) = 1 / (1 + 10^(-(Elo_Home - Elo_Away + HFA) / 400))
where $HFA \approx 24.0$ Elo points (~53.3% regular season home win rate). The margin multiplier scales $k$-factor:
multiplier = (abs(run_diff) + 3)^0.8 / (7.5 + 0.006 * abs(elo_diff))
Season-to-season regression reverts ratings 33% toward 1500 to account for roster turnover.

3. Regular Season Real Prices vs Postseason Fair-Coin Proxy (Spec §1, §4, §32)

A core architectural requirement of MLBComp: Regular Season (REG) strategies are evaluated against verified real closing odds from cesar-dx/mlb-betting-ml (15,442 games 2019-2025). Postseason (POST / WC / DS / LCS / WS) results are reported as a clearly labeled Fair-Coin Proxy:
ROI_proxy = 2 * (Win% - 50%)
representing model predictive skill against a hypothetical zero-vig +100 market. Postseason results are never merged with regular-season results without preserving the explicit breakdown.

4. Bivariate Poisson Scoring Grid & Quarter-Kelly Risk Management

Joint run scoring distributions are calculated via bivariate Poisson convolution for home and away expected lambdas ($λ_H, λ_A$):
P(Score_H = x, Score_A = y) = [ (λ_H^x e^(-λ_H) / x!) ] * [ (λ_A^y e^(-λ_A) / y!) ]
Quarter-Kelly staking determines stake size on confirmed positive expectation ($p \cdot b > 1$):
f* = (b*p - q) / b * 0.25
capped at a maximum of 5% of active bankroll per wager.

5. Zero-Hallucination Mandate & Data Provenance (Spec §31, §35)

Every game, line, score, and pitcher record is directly traceable to verified primary datasets. Quarantined mirror postseason fabrication is documented and permanently isolated in IRREGULARITIES.md. Reconstructed 2019-2024 postseason winners carry documented confidence tags. All 18 automated audit checks PASSED.
